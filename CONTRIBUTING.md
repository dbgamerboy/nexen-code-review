# Contributing to NEXEN

NEXEN is owner-controlled. Repository access, contributor credit and pull-request
approval do not grant equity, partnership rights, royalties or a commercial license.

Before accepting contributor-owned work into an owner-controlled release, verify
a signed rights assignment or another sufficient legal basis. The DENIZEN draft in
legal/NEXEN_DENIZEN_AGREEMENT_DRAFT.md is unsigned and is not that proof.
Preserve honest commit attribution; do not falsify authorship to imply ownership.

Work only on the accepted task and owned files. Preserve current instructions,
leases, unfinished changes, tests and STOP controls. This legal-documents update
does not authorize runtime changes, new spending, public release or broad code intake.
Disclose background IP, third-party licenses and material AI assistance. Do not upload
credentials, personal archives, identity documents or signed agreements.

Submit bounded changes for owner review. Maintain the filtered review-snapshot
boundary in REVIEW-SCOPE.md. Follow legal/README.md for the signing checklist.
